## Design Context

### Users
macOS power users who rely on the keyboard to launch apps and rapidly switch among apps, windows, and browser tabs without breaking focus.

### Brand Personality
Fast, restrained, and tool-like.

### Aesthetic Direction
A native macOS utility that stays visually quiet until invoked, then gives immediate, legible feedback. Motion should feel decisive and consistent rather than decorative.

### Design Principles
- Keep high-frequency keyboard interactions immediate and non-blocking.
- Use one short, repeatable entrance cue to explain where the overlay came from.
- Preserve native macOS materials, controls, localization, and accessibility behavior.
- Prefer progressive disclosure: keep window switching simple by default and expose compatible app tabs through an explicit setting.
- Respect Reduce Motion and avoid layout-driven animation that can flicker or shake.
